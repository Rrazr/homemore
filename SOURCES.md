Concept estimate: the unit count is a geometric fit of the selected design's footprint inside the user-drawn boundary. The 19 x 16 ft double-wide footprint and the cabin's 7 ft porch depth are derived from published figures, not published themselves. People housed is a per-style planning assumption (one per bedroom or private room; two for the Boxabl studio; four for the RHU per its maker's family-of-four-to-five figure); no occupancy rating is sourced. The non-Out-of-the-Box sources were reviewed September 18-19, 2026 through page summaries, so confirm the figures on each page before quoting them. Layout assumptions are ours, not sourced: 5 ft default clearance to the boundary, a 20 ft access aisle in front of every door (a fire-access placeholder for the civil engineer to verify), 10 ft between back-to-back rows, and a 2.65-metre model wall height. No verified parking loss, utility capacity, operator commitment, unit occupancy, costs, zoning approval or fire compliance. Boundary area is an approximate calculation from user map clicks.
