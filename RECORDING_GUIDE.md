# 90-second pitch demo

Keep the full MVP plan unchanged. This prototype demonstrates the intended journey with a prepared twelve-unit concept.

## Suggested video sequence

| Time | On screen | Suggested narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15 sec | Begin on the map | Faith communities have space and a desire to help. The first step toward housing is often the hardest. |
| 15–30 sec | Click around the dashed study area, then Finish | Start with a familiar property and outline the area you want to explore. |
| 30–50 sec | Generate housing concept, then slowly orbit | This illustrative concept shows how Fast-Set Boxes could help leadership picture a housing community. |
| 50–65 sec | Show the unit footprint and review requirements | The conversation includes practical questions about access, utilities, parking and local approvals. |
| 65–80 sec | Prepare your next steps, export presentation | Take a draft concept to your board, along with a focused brief for professional review. |
| 80–90 sec | Show the expert contacts | Sacred Spaces, Safe Homes helps churches take an informed first step with the people qualified to guide what comes next. |

## Recording tips

- Open the demo in a full desktop browser window. A 1440×900 or larger viewport works well.
- Load it once before recording so map tiles and the 3D library are cached.
- Use Restart for a clean take. Use sample boundary is a shortcut for rehearsals.
- Draw around the dashed example rectangle within Lot C. The sample is a study area, not the entire parking lot or a legal parcel boundary.
- Pause Slow orbit before selecting a fixed camera angle. Top view and Perspective offer repeatable views.
- The presentation download is an HTML slide deck. Open it in a browser and use the arrow keys; print to PDF if needed.
- Downloads and image capture are functional. Generation is a scripted concept reveal, not live zoning analysis or optimization.
- The app does not record your screen, microphone or camera.
